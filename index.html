Learn & Play Craps

Craps for iPhone and iPad. Play at a real layout with play money, learn the game a roll at a time, and see how a strategy really does over thousands of sessions.

Ways to play

  • Table

    Play with chips at a real layout. Buy in, set your bets, roll, and color up when you’re done. Before you roll, What if shows what every total would do to the bets on the felt. With Apple Music, one of three themed playlists plays while you’re at the table: Vegas lounge, High roller, or Late night at the tables.

  • Simulate

    One shooter or thousands. Write down what you’d bet on every roll and watch how it goes.

  • Compare

    Two strategies, same dice. See which one holds up when the luck is identical.

  • Sweep

    What one setting changes: the odds you take, the number of come bets, where you stop.

Learn the game

Tell it how well you know craps the first time you open it, and it starts you in the right place: straight into your first hand if you’re new. Twelve short lessons, most of them played on the real table with the dice set for you, cover the 36 ways two dice land, how a hand works, come and place bets, the field, the dark side, hardways, the middle of the table, buying and laying, pressing a bet and taking it down, the words you’ll hear, and how to behave at a real table. Along the way you call rolls before they land, and replay one to see how it could have gone.

At the table it keeps teaching: a short tip the first time something happens, like your first point or your first seven out, a sentence after each roll saying what it did to your bets, and, the first time you make an expensive bet, what the house keeps and any cheaper way to the same number. Any of them can be turned off in Settings.

Play money only

Point Made is a game and a teacher. No real money is ever wagered or won, and nothing in it can be cashed out.
