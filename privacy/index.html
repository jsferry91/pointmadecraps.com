Privacy Policy

Point Made: Learn & Play Craps

Last updated 26 November 2026

Point Made: Learn & Play Craps (“Point Made”, “the app”) is made by Jordan Ferry, an independent developer (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what information the app uses, who handles it, and the choices you have.

In short: I don’t run any servers for the app, there are no accounts, and I never see your games, sessions or strategies. The free version shows ads from Google, which uses some information about your device to show and measure them, within the choices you make. Pro is bought through Apple. Your progress syncs through your own iCloud account.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show ads, limit how often you see them, and measure them, Google may collect:

  • your device’s IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out;
  • information about your device and the app, such as the device model, operating system, language and settings;
  • how you interact with ads, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped;
  • diagnostic information about how ads load and perform; and
  • your device’s advertising identifier, only if you allow it when Apple asks whether the app may track you.

Your choices:

  • Tracking. The app asks Apple’s tracking question once, after your first game. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the advertising identifier isn’t used and ads may be less relevant. You can change your answer any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent. Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choices in the app with Ad privacy choices, under Go Pro or in the session panel at the table.
  • Pro. With Pro, the app shows no ads and requests none. When the app opens with Pro, Google’s ad service isn’t started at all.

Google handles this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. I receive only overall reports from AdMob, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned, not information about you.

Buying Pro

Pro is an in-app purchase made through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and your payment details; I never see them. The app only learns from the App Store whether Pro is owned on your Apple Account, so that it can turn Pro on, including on your other devices and for your family through Family Sharing. Apple’s handling of purchases is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

iCloud sync

If you’re signed in to iCloud, the app keeps these in step across your devices, stored in your own iCloud account:

  • the lessons you’ve finished in Learn, and how well you said you know craps;
  • the table and strategies you last set up;
  • your history of sessions at the table; and
  • the strategies you save.

This data is stored by Apple in your private iCloud storage. I can’t see it. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for Point Made in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. To delete what’s stored there, use Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Table music and Apple Music

Table music is off unless you choose a playlist in Settings or at the table. When you do, the app asks for access to Apple Music, and with it, it:

  • asks Apple Music whether your account can play songs, so it can tell you if it can’t;
  • looks up the songs on its own lists in the Apple Music catalog, and plays them while you’re at a table;
  • lists the playlists in your Apple Music library when you open Your playlists, so you can choose one, and plays the one you choose; and
  • adds one of its playlists to your Apple Music library, but only when you tap Save to my Apple Music.

The playlist you choose is remembered on your device by its name and Apple Music ID. The app doesn’t read what you listen to, and nothing about your music comes to me. Apple handles Apple Music under Apple’s Privacy Policy. To stop it, choose Off under Table music, or turn off Media & Apple Music for Point Made in the Settings app.

On your device only

A game in progress, your daily allowance and credits, and settings such as sound, casino ambience, haptics, table music and the table design are kept only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing a result

When you share a simulation result, it goes through the iOS share sheet to wherever you choose to send it. The app doesn’t send it anywhere else.

What the app doesn’t do

  • It has no accounts and asks for no name, email address or contact details.
  • It has no analytics or crash reporting of its own.
  • It doesn’t use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • I don’t sell your information, and I don’t receive any to sell.

No real money, and children

Point Made uses play money only: nothing is wagered, won or paid out. Because it simulates a casino game, it is meant for adults and isn’t directed at children. I don’t knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you have a concern, email me.

This website

pointmadecraps.com uses no cookies, analytics, or third-party scripts or fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses to keep the service secure, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information about you, or to object to how it’s used. I don’t hold information about you myself. For information Google holds in connection with ads, you can use Google’s tools at My Ad Center or contact Google. For anything else, or if you aren’t sure, email me and I’ll help.

Changes

If the app starts using information in a new way, I’ll update this policy before that version of the app is released and change the date at the top.
