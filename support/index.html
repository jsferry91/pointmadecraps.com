Restoring Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase that belongs to your Apple Account, so it works on every iPhone and iPad signed in to that account. On a new device it usually turns on by itself. If it doesn’t:

  1. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.
  2. In Point Made, open Go Pro from the home screen.
  3. Tap Bought it before? Restore Pro.

Pro is shared with your family through Family Sharing, if you use it.

Questions

What does Pro include?

No ads anywhere in the app, and no daily limits: buy in, add chips and run simulations as often as you like. It’s a single purchase, not a subscription.

Is any real money involved?

No. Every chip in Point Made is play money. Nothing is wagered, won, or paid out, and nothing in the app can be exchanged for money or prizes.

Why does the app ask to track me?

The free version shows ads from Google. Apple asks whether those ads may use your device’s advertising identifier to be more relevant to you. The app asks once, after your first game, and works exactly the same whichever you choose. You can change it later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law asks for it, such as in the European Economic Area and the UK, the app asks for your consent before showing ads. To change your answer, open Go Pro or the session panel at the table and tap Ad privacy choices.

Does my progress follow me to another device?

Yes, through your own iCloud account: the lessons you’ve finished, your session history, your saved strategies, and the table and strategies you last set up. A game in progress and your daily allowance stay on the device you’re playing on.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad, on iOS and iPadOS 26.5 or later.
